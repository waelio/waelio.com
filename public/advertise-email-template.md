Companies that sell developer tools are the best fit. Examples:

- Developer tool startups (hosting, databases, APIs)
- SaaS companies targeting engineers
- npm package competitors or complements
- Cloudflare, Vercel, Supabase, PlanetScale type companies (start smaller first)
- Any company with a "developers" page on their site

# How to find their email

1. Go to their website
2. Look for: Contact / Press / Partnerships / Advertise
3. Or find someone on LinkedIn with title: "Developer Relations", "Growth", "Marketing"
