Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information waelio.com collects, how it is used, and how it is protected when you visit the site or sign in to access the private workspace.

Information we collect

  • Google Sign-In data: if you sign in, we receive basic account information needed to authenticate you, such as your name, email address, and Google identity token claims.
  • Session data: after successful sign-in, we store a secure session cookie so you can stay signed in.
  • Technical data: like most websites, basic request information such as IP address, browser details, and server logs may be processed for reliability, security, and troubleshooting.
  • Public package requests: when you browse package stats, the site fetches public metadata from npm services.

How we use information

  • To operate and secure waelio.com.
  • To verify approved users for the private workspace.
  • To maintain active sessions after sign-in.
  • To diagnose errors, protect the service, and improve reliability.

Cookies and authentication

waelio.com uses a session cookie after successful sign-in. This cookie is used only to keep you authenticated.

Advertising

waelio.com displays ads served by Google AdSense. AdSense may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Consent (EEA / GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, waelio.com will ask for your consent before enabling advertising cookies or analytics tracking. You can change your choice at any time by clicking the "Privacy choices" button at the bottom of any page.

Without your consent, only essential cookies required for basic site functionality are used. No personalized ads will be served.

Third-party services

The site relies on third-party services including Google Sign-In for authentication, Google AdSense for advertising, Google Analytics for site measurement, and npm services for public package metadata and download statistics. Those services may process data according to their own policies.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by hosting, infrastructure, authentication, or security providers only as needed to operate the service.

Data retention

Authentication and session information is retained only as long as needed to provide access, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations. Session cookies expire automatically.

Your choices and rights

  • You can choose not to sign in to the private workspace.
  • You can sign out at any time to clear the active session.
  • You may use only an approved Google account to access private content.
  • You can accept or reject advertising and analytics cookies via the consent banner.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads at Google Ads Settings.
  • If you are in the EEA, you have the right to access, rectify, or request deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
